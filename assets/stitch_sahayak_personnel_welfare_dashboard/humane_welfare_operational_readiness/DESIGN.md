## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a paradigm shift for uniformed personnel monitoring: transitioning from clinical oversight and surveillance to institutional accompaniment, psychological safety, and dignified welfare. Tailored for command leadership, medical officers, and personnel within high-stress defense and policing environments, the interface creates an atmosphere of institutional gravity stripped of punitive anxiety.

The visual style unites **Corporate Institutionalism** with **Empathetic Humanism**. It avoids clinical coldness, punitive "traffic-light" urgency, or military-surveillance aesthetics. Instead, it relies on serene porcelain backdrops, deep navy anchoring, and restorative oceanic teals. The emotional goal is immediate decompression: officers and commanders interacting with the system must feel safeguarded, respected, and supported by their institution. Data is presented transparently and ethically, prioritizing aggregate cohort health over individual policing.

## Colors

The color palette is engineered to remove threat triggers while sustaining clear triage legibility across personnel welfare indicators.

### Primary, Supportive, and Institutional Accents
- **Supportive Teal (`#2A9D8F`)**: The foundational seed color representing baseline homeostasis, replenishment, and resilience. Paired with a delicate tint (`#E8F5F3`) for safe-state fills and restful card accents.
- **Deep Navy Blue (`#1E3A5F` / `#142842`)**: Conveys institutional gravitas, constitutional trust, and disciplined security. Used for persistent primary navigation, high-level command structures, and structural boundaries.
- **Official Brass Gold (`#C59B27`)**: Applied with restraint for institutional insignias, statutory verifications, and emblem badges to signify state trust.

### Welfare Status Tiers (Non-Punitive Scaling)
- **Balanced / Stable State**: Primary Supportive Teal (`#2A9D8F`), fill `#E8F5F3`. Signifies steady emotional and physiological equilibrium.
- **Observation / Watch State**: Warm Amber (`#F4A261`), fill `#FEF6EE`. Replaces harsh caution markers with an attentive, warm alert indicating heightened operational tempo.
- **Support Recommended State**: Muted Coral (`#E76F51`), fill `#FDF0ED`. Deliberately replaces aggressive crimson/red to avoid the psychological framing of disciplinary fault or failure, framing elevated strain as a collective logistical cue for rest and support.

### Canvas, Surface, & Typography
- **Canvas / App Backdrop**: Cool Porcelain (`#F7F9FB`) to eliminate eye strain during prolonged command shifts.
- **Surface / Containers**: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) with whisper-quiet boundary lines (`#E2E8F0`).
- **Text Primary**: Charcoal Navy (`#1E293B`) ensuring AAA contrast against white cards.
- **Text Secondary & Meta**: Muted Slate (`#64748B`) for context labels, timestamps, and secondary readings.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy is intentionally structured around accessibility, legibility in high-stress command rooms, and absolute clarity.

- **Headlines (`Atkinson Hyperlegible Next`)**: Selected for its hyper-distinct letterforms, designed specifically to disambiguate similar characters under cognitive fatigue or low-light field terminal conditions. It imparts a reassuring, humanistic quality without sacrificing structural strength.
- **Body & Metrics (`Public Sans`)**: A clean, institutional, highly readable workhorse that brings neutrality, crisp numerical tabulation, and open counterforms to dense operational reports.
- **Microcopy Guidelines**: Numerical data points (e.g., resting heart-rate trends, sleep quality indexes) should always use tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`). Avoid aggressive capitalization; labels use uppercase sparingly with expanded tracking solely for category chips and status badges.

## Layout & Spacing

The platform is designed around a responsive desktop-first framework targeting 1440px wide screens, adapting gracefully to laptop displays (1280px) and field tablets (768px - 1024px).

### The Spatial Grid
- **Desktop Grid (1440px)**: A 12-column fluid grid governed by `2rem` outer page margins and `1.5rem` (`24px`) gutters. A fixed left-hand institutional navigation rail occupies 260px, allowing the remaining viewport to distribute operational cards cleanly across 3-column or 4-column metric clusters.
- **Tablet / Split View (768px - 1023px)**: Condenses to an 8-column system with `1rem` gutters; navigation shifts to a persistent collapsible rail or top banner.
- **Mobile Handheld (360px - 767px)**: Transforms into a single-column stacked welfare overview with `1rem` outer canvas padding.

### Rhythm Principles
Whitespace is treated as a psychological buffer. Rather than packing dashboards with high-density data sheets, widgets maintain generous internal padding (`space-lg` / `24px`), giving command officers mental room to absorb personnel well-being trends calmly.

## Elevation & Depth

To avoid oppressive, heavy shadowing that can subconsciously amplify crisis sentiment, this system uses low-contrast outlines coupled with ambient, feather-light shadows.

### Surface Elevation Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Canvas Base)**: Cool porcelain `#F7F9FB`. Ground level for non-interactive backgrounds.
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Resting)**: Pure White `#FFFFFF` with a single-pixel hairline border (`1px solid #E2E8F0`) and an ambient tinted drop shadow: `0 1px 3px rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.04), 0 4px 12px rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.02)`. 
- **Level 2 (Active / Hover / Context Menus)**: Elevated White `#FFFFFF` with soft teal diffusion: `0 8px 24px rgba(42, 157, 143, 0.08), 0 2px 6px rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.04)`, border shifting to `#CBD5E1`.
- **Level 3 (Modals / Intervention Dialogs)**: Pure white elevated above an institutional overlay (`rgba(20, 40, 66, 0.45)` with a `4px` backdrop blur). Shadow: `0 20px 40px -8px rgba(20, 40, 66, 0.16)`.

Borders remain soft throughout, preventing punitive box-like enclosures and fostering an approachable workspace.

## Shapes

The geometry of the interface actively mitigates severity. Strict, razor-sharp edges are avoided entirely, as clinical and military environments can feel rigid and disciplinary.

- **Primary Cards and Major Canvas Panes**: Configured with `rounded-xl` geometry (equivalent to 12px), imparting stability and soft boundaries.
- **Interactive Form Inputs and Buttons**: Styled at an ergonomic medium radius (8px), balancing precise interactive affordance with gentle corners.
- **Status Badges, Indicator Pills, and Filter Chips**: Sculpted with continuous pill curvature (`9999px`), transforming diagnostic metrics into organic, welcoming tags.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Support Button**: Solid Supportive Teal (`#2A9D8F`) with white text and `8px` rounded corners. Hover transitions to `#238276`. Focus ring uses an accessible dual outline (`2px white` inner, `2px #2A9D8F` outer).
- **Institutional Secondary Button**: Bordered with `#1E3A5F` (Navy) at `1px solid`, background transparent, text `#1E3A5F`. Subtle fill `#F1F5F9` on hover.
- **Gentle Action / Tertiary Button**: Text-only or ghost button with muted slate coloration, transforming to `#E8F5F3` tinted background on hover. Used for dismissals and non-urgent drill-downs.

### Status Indicators & Welfare Chips
- **Geometry**: Fully pill-shaped (`border-radius: 9999px`) with padding `4px 12px`.
- **Stable Pill**: Background `#E8F5F3`, text `#1D6F65`, featuring a soft `6px` solid dot in `#2A9D8F`. Label reads "Optimal Readiness" or "Stable".
- **Attentive Pill**: Background `#FEF6EE`, text `#B25E1B`, dot `#F4A261`. Label reads "Rest Recommended" or "Monitoring".
- **Support Pill**: Background `#FDF0ED`, text `#A43B22`, dot `#E76F51`. Label reads "Support Scheduled" or "Care Priority". Avoid words like "Critical", "Deficient", or "Failing".

### Cards & Container Panels
- Rendered in `#FFFFFF`, bordered with `1px solid #E2E8F0`, with `12px` roundedness.
- Top headers include subtle contextual meta-text in Muted Slate (`#64748B`) paired with high-legibility Atkinson Hyperlegible Next section titles.
- Action items are decoupled from disciplinary actions; they frame insights around shift rotation, hydration, counselling availability, and decompression intervals.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Background `#FFFFFF` with `1px solid #CBD5E1`, transitioning to `#2A9D8F` on active focus accompanied by a subtle `#2A9D8F15` aura. Height standardized at 40px for desktop clarity.
- Checkboxes and radios employ rounded-soft geometry with `#2A9D8F` active fills, signaling care and agreement rather than rigid compliance audits.

### Cohort Anonymization & Aggregation Visualizers
- Stress and wellness metric graphs omit individual punitive callouts. Visual trends are grouped by unit, shift, or operational cycle using soft area fills under teal, amber, and coral curvature charts.
- Threshold triggers do not sound harsh visual sirens; they introduce calming banner strips with gentle prompt options: *"Unit 4B has exceeded sustained high-tempo hours. Would you like to schedule an operational rest rotation?"*
